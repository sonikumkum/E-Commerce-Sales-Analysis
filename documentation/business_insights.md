# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data for 2023 using Python and MySQL.

The objective is to identify revenue trends, top-performing products and categories, customer behavior, city-wise performance, and preferred payment methods.

The analysis contains **1,200 orders from 537 unique customers**, with **2,546 units sold** and total revenue of approximately **₹3.63 crore**.

---

## 2. Key Performance Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 1,200 |
| Unique Customers | 537 |
| Total Units Sold | 2,546 |
| Total Revenue | ₹3,63,31,900 |
| Average Order Value | ₹30,276.58 |

---

## 3. Key Business Insights

### 3.1 Monthly Sales Trend

September 2023 was the highest-revenue month.

- Revenue: **₹81,86,800**
- Orders: **266**
- Units Sold: **555**

September generated approximately **22.5% of annual revenue**, indicating a strong seasonal sales period.

**Business implication:**  
Inventory, marketing campaigns, and promotional activities should be planned ahead of high-demand periods.

---

### 3.2 Product Performance

The **Premium Laptop** was the highest-revenue product.

- Orders: **73**
- Units Sold: **159**
- Revenue: **₹1,28,79,000**
- Average Units per Order: **2.18**

The **Smartphone** was the second-highest revenue-generating product with approximately **₹1.26 crore** in revenue.

**Business implication:**  
High-value electronics should receive priority in inventory planning, promotions, and cross-selling strategies.

---

### 3.3 Category Performance

Electronics was the strongest-performing category.

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Electronics | ₹2,96,10,000 |
| Home & Kitchen | ₹33,47,000 |
| Sports | ₹19,05,000 |
| Beauty | ₹8,60,800 |
| Fashion | ₹6,21,400 |

Electronics contributed approximately **81.5% of total revenue**.

**Business implication:**  
The business is heavily dependent on Electronics, so maintaining product availability in this category is critical. At the same time, lower-performing categories can be developed through targeted promotions.

---

### 3.4 City-wise Performance

**Delhi** was the highest-revenue city.

- Revenue: **₹56,46,800**
- Orders: **196**
- Customers: **159**
- Units Sold: **434**

The next strongest markets were **Chennai** and **Jaipur**.

**Business implication:**  
Marketing campaigns and inventory allocation can be prioritized in high-performing cities while targeted campaigns can be used to improve sales in lower-performing markets.

---

### 3.5 Payment Method Analysis

**UPI** was the leading payment method.

| Payment Method | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| UPI | ₹1,22,73,300 |
| Credit Card | ₹81,93,000 |
| Debit Card | ₹74,20,400 |
| Net Banking | ₹51,66,100 |
| Cash on Delivery | ₹32,79,100 |

UPI generated approximately **₹1.23 crore**, making it the largest revenue-generating payment method.

**Business implication:**  
The business should continue optimizing the UPI checkout experience and consider UPI-based promotional offers.

---

### 3.6 Customer Segment Analysis

New customers generated the highest revenue.

| Customer Segment | Customers | Orders | Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New | 347 | 542 | ₹1,68,57,000 |
| Returning | 303 | 409 | ₹1,12,10,000 |
| Loyal | 203 | 249 | ₹82,65,200 |

New customers contributed approximately **46.4% of total revenue**.

**Business implication:**  
Customer acquisition is performing well, but converting new customers into returning and loyal customers can improve long-term customer value.

---

### 3.7 Repeat Customer Analysis

The analysis identified customers with multiple orders and ranked them based on their total revenue contribution.

Some high-value repeat customers placed **4–8 orders**, demonstrating the presence of recurring purchasing behavior.

**Business implication:**  
The business can strengthen customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized offers, product recommendations, and repeat-purchase incentives.

---

## 4. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Prepare for Seasonal Demand

Increase inventory and marketing activity before high-performing months such as September.

### Recommendation 2 — Prioritize High-Value Electronics

Maintain strong availability of Premium Laptops, Smartphones, and other high-value electronics.

### Recommendation 3 — Improve Customer Retention

Use loyalty programs, personalized discounts, and follow-up campaigns to convert new customers into returning customers.

### Recommendation 4 — Focus on High-Performing Cities

Prioritize Delhi, Chennai, and Jaipur for targeted campaigns and inventory planning.

### Recommendation 5 — Promote Digital Payments

Since UPI generates the highest revenue, UPI-based offers and a streamlined digital checkout process can be used to encourage adoption.

### Recommendation 6 — Develop Lower-Performing Categories

Use targeted promotions and bundles to increase sales in Fashion, Beauty, and other lower-revenue categories.

---

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis shows that **Electronics is the primary revenue driver**, while **September represents the strongest sales period**.

High-value products such as Premium Laptops and Smartphones contribute significantly to revenue, while Delhi, Chennai, and Jaipur are important geographic markets.

The business can improve future performance by combining seasonal inventory planning, targeted marketing, customer retention strategies, and digital payment optimization.
